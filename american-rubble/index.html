The Anatomy of Collapse • Album 18

American Rubble

The Anatomy of Collapse, Civil Conflict & The Human Cost of Division

"A civil war is never a good thing. It is the most devastating wound a nation can inflict upon itself. We seem to have forgotten the reality of our own past... 'American Rubble' is my response to that dangerous fantasy."

Serving as the horrifying prognosis to the diagnosis delivered in Divide and Conquer, this concept album confronts the listener with the universal, non-partisan human cost of societal collapse. A meticulous four-act descent tracing the breakdown of electrical and food logistics, intimate neighborhood militarization, black-market warlordism, and transgenerational trauma.

A fire alarm in the night—grounded in real-world supply chain and infrastructure research, designed to leave every listener with one haunting question: Was it worth it?
