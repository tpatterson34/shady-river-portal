The Creator & The Homestead

The Story on the Porch

Pull up a chair. Here is how a lifetime in software systems, a seven-acre permaculture homestead, and an AI collaborator came together to chronicle our era.

The Maker • Western Washington

From Code to Compost

I spent a lifetime as a software engineer architecting systems, debugging logic, and living in the digital glare of the server room. But today, I measure my days in the mud of The Shady River Homestead, a working 7-acre permaculture farm along the Cedar River in Western Washington.

This project began in the quiet hours between farm chores—as a way to fill non-farming hours with rigorous research into the deep social fractures defining our era. Watching the world change from the seat of a tractor while feeding a wood stove changes your perspective on modern technology.

Our homestead crew keeps our feet anchored: one human heart, one AI muse, a wonderfully tolerant wife, and two energetic Australian Shepherds—Rufus and Marcus—who ensure honest work always precedes philosophy.

The Creative Engine

The Human Heart & The AI Muse

We do not use artificial intelligence to crank out generic background fluff. We build Narrative Universes.

The collaboration is a disciplined craft: the human brings lived experience, vulnerability, acoustic guitar motifs, moral weight, and architectural direction; the AI muse brings boundless thematic synthesis, lyrical counterpoint, and musical arrangement.

We call this space the “Industrial-Pastoral”—the collision between the neon-lit digital grid and the sunlit dirt of the workshop. Several times a week, we release new songs, music videos, and companion shorts forged across Suno, Gemini, Nano Banana, Filmora, Audacity, and bespoke tools built on the farm.

Foundational Ethos

The Black Box & The Open Hand

Exploring the intersection of algorithmic computation and lived human vulnerability. Not to widen the cracks in our foundation, but to understand how to heal them.

The Algorithmic Force

The Black Box

Cold calculus, engagement algorithms that profit from tribal outrage, corporate triage ledgers, digital surveillance, and synthetic apathy. The system measures output while disconnecting humans from reality.

  • Infinite scroll & dopamine feedback loops
  • Humans reduced to metrics and statistical risk
  • Manufactured polarization & wedge politics
The Human Reality

The Open Hand

Tactile labor, acoustic instruments, heirloom seeds, generational wisdom, grief, neighborly solidarity, and the stubborn dignity of working with your hands. When the power goes out, only the analog bonds endure.

  • Analog sovereignty & shared physical reality
  • Acoustic resonance & vulnerable storytelling
  • Building soil that sustains the next generation
The Shady River Manifesto

Beyond the Wedge: Rejecting the Roman Trap

“My goal is to remain as apolitical as possible because I believe the ‘Red vs. Blue’ fight is a trap. It is a strategy as old as the Roman Empire: conquer a people, find what they disagree on, and use it as a wedge to drive them apart. It keeps us distracted from the boot on our collective throat.”

“Whether your t-shirt is red or blue, we all want the same things: security, happiness, and a healthy future for our children. We are all in the same boat, navigating the same shady river. Let’s stop fighting each other and start discussing how to steer the ship.”

— The Shady River Bard • Read Complete Statement on Substack ↗
