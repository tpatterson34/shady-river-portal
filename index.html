The Land & The Soil

The Shady River Homestead, LLC

"The Soil Beneath the Songs"

The music of The Shady River Bard is not composed in a sterile urban studio. It is forged in the dirt, timber, and rain of a working permaculture homestead in Western Washington.

When a song like The Farmer's Broken Back or This Sacred Ground speaks of agrarian sacrifice, rural hospital closures, and the generational fight for dignity, it draws from living observation: hands calloused by tractor steel, soil biology nurtured through seasonal cycles, and deep community solidarity.

Regenerative Soil
