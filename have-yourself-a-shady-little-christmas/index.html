Have Yourself a
Shady Little Christmas

A Social Realist Modern Christmas Carol

“The opposite of addiction is not sobriety. It's connection.”

Welcome to Have Yourself a Shady Little Christmas, an album that is not a celebration of the holiday, but an unflinching look at the “shady” paradox at its heart: the stark, cold reality of those living in the shadows of our festive light. Structured as a modern Christmas carol told through the lens of social realism, the 15 movements trace the journey of William—from social invisibility and the bitter struggle for survival against a lethal winter, through the harrowing roulette of the fentanyl crisis and emergency revival, to an unexpected encounter with Joe the store Santa, and the 76/32 statistical miracle of radical human connection.

Accessibility Statement

The Shady River Bard digital portal is committed to providing an inclusive and accessible experience for all listeners, researchers, and visitors.

This subsite has been designed in strict accordance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA requirements:

  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators.
  • Screen-reader optimized ARIA landmarks, roles, and live regions.
  • User-controlled high contrast, dyslexia-friendly fonts, and typography scaling.
  • Strict adherence to prefers-reduced-motion for all animations.
  • Descriptive alternative text for all 31 album artwork assets.
  • Zero chords in published lyrics for assistive readability.

If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact our web accessibility team at contact@theshadyriverbard.com.
