THE POISON WE TRUST

A Social Analysis of Modern Public Health & The Body Politic

The modern health crisis is not an accident of genetics or a failure of willpower; it is the predictable outcome of institutional capture.

The Poison We Trust is an intentional 4-movement concept album by The Shady River Bard. Set to gritty Americana, country blues, and atmospheric folk, it investigates the dietary deceptions, pharmaceutical profiteering, and regulatory entanglements that have compromised the American body politic, before charting an empowering counter-current toward health sovereignty, regenerative nutrition, and earned hope.
