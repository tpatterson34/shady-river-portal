This Sacred Ground

From Creation and Betrayal to Defiant Resilience on Ancestral Earth

"The circle was broken. But the ground... the ground remembers. The ground endures. It holds the dust of our ancestors and the seeds of our children. It is here. It is holy. It is home."

— The Shady River Bard, This Sacred Ground Liner Notes

This Sacred Ground is a 5-act folk epic bearing witness to the Native American experience. Conceived as a journey through myth, historical trauma, and cultural resurgence, the narrative moves from the mythic harmony of pre-colonial creation through broken treaties, forced removal, and the cultural erasure of boarding schools, culminating in a triumphant celebration of language revitalization and modern Indigenous sovereignty.
