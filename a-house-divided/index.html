A HOUSE DIVIDED

A Narrative and Thematic Analysis for a Concept Album

From the searing inferno of “The Roaring Fire” to the haunting silence of “Ghost Town,” A House Divided navigates the landscapes of environmental and political division.

Rather than treating polarization as an insurmountable chasm, this ambitious 6-movement concept album by The Shady River Bard investigates the physical reality of an unquiet earth, the architecture of manufactured doubt, and symmetrical human loss across rural and working-class communities—before charting an empowering turn toward common ground, regenerative agriculture, and unlikely alliances.
