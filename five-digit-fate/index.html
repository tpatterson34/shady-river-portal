Five-Digit Fate

A Social Cartography of American Poverty, Geographic Determinism & The Unbranded Heart

In modern America, your five-digit zip code is not merely an address—it has become your corporate brand identity, stamped at birth and seared into your prospects. Grounded in the empirical research of Raj Chetty’s Opportunity Atlas (tracking 20M Americans across Census tracts), Anne Case & Angus Deaton’s Deaths of Despair, and Richard Rothstein’s The Color of Law, this 17-track epic travels through five iconic landscapes: the cotton rows of the Mississippi Delta, the abandoned coal hollows of Appalachia, the redlined avenues of Chicago, the deindustrialized ruins of Detroit, and the speculative gentrification of Oakland.

"Before your first breath, a number was assigned. Not a name whispered by your mother, but a five-digit fate... It's the ceiling they build just above your head, and the floor that's designed to fall away."
— Track 01: Prologue (A National Static)
