Unequal Justice

Systemic Inequity, Mass Incarceration & The Scale of Law

In a world of fleeting headlines and endless noise, Unequal Justice is an unflinching 18-track narrative folk-blues epic examining the two Americas living under one flag. Guided by the ancient metaphor of the "two-weight stone"—one weight used when weighing out justice for the powerful, and another when measuring out punishment for the poor—the album dissects corporate impunity, the cash-bail meat grinder, mandatory sentencing, and the permanent stigma of a criminal record, before rising in defiance with exonerations, whistleblowers, and grassroots community solidarity.

The Album Thesis: The Two-Weight Stone

"The ancient proverb warns that a false balance is an abomination, but a just weight is delight. 'Unequal Justice' exposes modern legal systems operating with a two-weight stone—one of impunity, deferred prosecution, and golden parachutes for the wealthy, and another of cash bail, mandatory minimums, and generational ruin for the poor. Bearing witness to this disparity is the first, indispensable step toward demanding a single, true scale."
