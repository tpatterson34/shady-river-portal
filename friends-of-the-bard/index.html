The Scottish Literary Salon, Empirical Inquiries & Companion Compositions

Trevor writes deeply considered essays and prose interrogating constitutional covenants, the 1689 Claim of Right, the principle of salvo jure cujuslibet, and the manufactured divisions of contemporary society. His prose directly inspires political folk-rock melodies in DADGAD acoustic tuning with low whistle and cello.
