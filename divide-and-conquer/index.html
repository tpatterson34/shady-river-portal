Divide and Conquer

A Four-Act Dissection of Manufactured Division & The Path to Reconnection

Do you feel it? The constant, low-grade hum of anger in the air. The exhaustion of waking up to a new outrage every single day. The bewildering sense that the world is fracturing along lines you can no longer comprehend, turning neighbors into enemies and every conversation into a potential battlefield.

Conceived as the unifying theory for The Shady River Bard's entire 26-album discography, Divide and Conquer moves beyond chronicling the symptoms of American decline to dissecting the core mechanism: the deliberate, strategic fragmentation of the populace by a competing elite class.

Grounding its analysis in Peter Turchin's historical dynamics—the explosive collision of elite overproduction and popular immiseration—the narrative unmasks the media algorithms, revolving-door lobbying, and political tribalism engineered to keep our eyes locked on each other rather than upward.
