    Accessibility Statement

    Conformance Standard: Conformance with Level AA of the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 (WCAG 2.1 AA) and the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA Title III).

    Our Commitment

    The Shady River Bard is dedicated to ensuring that digital music, liner notes, and narrative research remain accessible to everyone, including individuals with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive disabilities.

    Key Accessibility Features

    • Full Keyboard Navigation: All cards, tabs, modal drawers, and interactive buttons are accessible via standard Tab, Enter, and Escape keystrokes with high-contrast visible focus indicators.
    • Screen Reader Optimization: Complete WAI-ARIA dialog patterns, accessible labels on all form inputs and interactive buttons, descriptive alt text on album artwork, and live region announcements.
    • Built-in Display Preferences: High-contrast palette toggles, text resizing up to 200% without loss of functionality, link underlining, and reduced-motion controls.
    • Skip Navigation: A direct "Skip to main content" link for keyboard users.

    Feedback & Immediate Assistance

    We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this catalog. If you encounter any barriers or need assistance accessing any tracklist, liner notes, or concept documentation, please email us directly:

    theshadyriverbard@gmail.com Send Email →

    We treat all accessibility inquiries as high priority and aim to respond and provide alternative accessible formats within 1 to 2 business days.
