Special credit and gratitude go to Swedish software developer, researcher, and commentator Vanessa Wingårdh (@VanessaWingardh). In her 11-minute investigative report, she began with a jarring personal message from an expat friend on Italy’s Sorrento coast staring at empty supermarket shelves—and pulled the thread across Europe, Asia, and North America.

Every verse, chorus, and visual tableau in “And the People Cry Out” translates her synthesis into a working-class solidarity anthem—and below, we verify and document the primary institutional sources behind every single claim.
